Featured Campaign

7-Day Plastic-Free Challenge

What is the problem?

Single-use plastics create large amounts of waste and can cause serious problems for wildlife and the environment.

Why does it matter?

Small changes in our everyday choices can reduce unnecessary plastic waste and help protect our planet.

What can you do?

For the next seven days, try to avoid single-use plastic. Use reusable bottles, bags and containers whenever possible.
