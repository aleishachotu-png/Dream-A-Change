About Us

Our Mission

Dream A Change is an awareness website created to help young people learn about important issues and discover ways they can make a positive difference.

Why We Created This Website

We believe that awareness is the first step towards positive change. Small actions can become powerful when many people participate.

Our Goal

Our goal is to share campaigns, encourage action and inspire young people to help their communities and the environment.
